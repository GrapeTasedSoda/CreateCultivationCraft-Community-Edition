# Create: Cultivation Craft — Community Edition

> Original mod by **[upo](https://modrinth.com/user/upo)** (MIT License) · Community Edition maintained by **GrapeTasedSoda**
>
> 原模组作者：**upo**（MIT 许可） · 社区版维护：**GrapeTasedSoda**

A Create addon with two big gameplay systems: the **Cultivation Tank**, a machine that plants, grows, harvests and replants crops on its own — and the **Greenhouse**, a sealed glass building where temperature and humidity are yours to control. In-game tutorials ship through Ponder: point at any machine and read.

一个 Create 附属模组，带来两大玩法系统：**栽培罐**——一台自己种菜、自己收菜的机器；**温室**——一座温湿度攥在手里的玻璃房。对准任何机器使用 Ponder 都有图文教程。

## English

Create: Cultivation Craft turns a farm into a factory. Everything runs on Create's rotation power, and the outputs drop straight into your existing logistics. Install it, craft a tank, and start with a wheat field — or go big and grow things no natural biome would allow.

### 🌱 The Cultivation Tank

- Stack a **Cultivation Tank** on a **Cultivation Base**, right-click the tank with any seed — the machine handles everything after that: it grows the crop, harvests it into the base's storage and keeps one item for replanting. As long as the storage has room, it never stops.
- Funnel or chute the outputs away and the farm becomes a production line.
- It grows vanilla crops, flowers, mushrooms, even sea pickles and chorus — and stacking plants like sugar cane, cactus, kelp and bamboo grow layer by layer: build the tank taller, harvest taller.
- The base has a dedicated fertilizer slot (bone meal, mod composts, or the mod's own Efficient Fertilizer, which can also be right-clicked on crops), and a Spout can water the tank for an extra boost. Two lamps tell you how the machine feels: red = storage almost full, orange = the tank is too short for this crop.
- Some crops have special tastes, wants to be watered with lava.

### 🏡 The Greenhouse

- Build a sealed room out of **Greenhouse Glass** and place a **Greenhouse Controller** inside. It scans the room, shows the live temperature and humidity, and lists every crop you are growing together with the climate it likes.
- Push the climate with powered devices: the **Air Conditioner** steers temperature, the **Humidifier** and **Dehumidifier** steer humidity. They chase your setpoints automatically — or press the hook button and the controller picks the best setpoints for your crops on its own.
- Crops in their ideal climate grow dramatically faster; outside their survival range they stall completely. The greenhouse is how you grow the impossible.
- The **Sprinkler** is a plug-and-play helper for any farm: hang it, feed it water, and it keeps the farmland below permanently hydrated.
- Greenhouse glass comes with a matching glass door, and season mods treat it as a proper greenhouse — seasonal crops keep growing indoors.

## 简体中文

Create: Cultivation Craft 把菜地变成流水线。一切吃 Create 的旋转动力，产物直接对接你现有的物流系统。装上模组、合成一个罐子，从一片麦田开始——或者玩大的，去种自然环境里种不出来的东西。

### 🌱 栽培罐

- 把**栽培罐**叠在**栽培基座**上，拿着种子对罐子右键——剩下的机器完成：自动生长、成熟后收进基座的仓库并自动留种补种。只要仓库放得下，它就一直转下去。
- 用漏斗或溜槽把产物抽走，菜地就成了生产线。
- 原版作物、花、蘑菇，甚至海泡菜和紫颂植物都能种；sugar cane、cactus、kelp、bamboo 这类 stacking 作物会一层层往上长——罐子叠多高，就收多高。
- 基座有专属肥料槽（bone meal、各模组堆肥，或本模组新增的 Efficient Fertilizer——它也能拿在手里对作物右键催熟），再用 Spout 浇浇水，效率更上一层楼。机器的状态写在两盏灯上：红灯 = 仓库快满，橙灯 = 罐子不够高。
- 有些作物口味特别，得用岩浆浇。

### 🏡 温室

- 用**温室玻璃**搭一间密封房，在里面放上**温室控制器**。它会扫描整个房间，实时显示温度与湿度，并把你种下的每种作物连同它喜欢的气候区间一起列出来。
- 用通电设备拨动气候：**Air Conditioner** 管温度，**Humidifier** 与 **Dehumidifier** 管湿度。它们会自动把房间推向你的设定值——也可以按下挂钩按钮，控制器会根据你的作物自己选好最佳设定值。
- 作物在理想气候里长得飞快；跌出生存区间则彻底停滞。温室，就是让你种出“原本种不了”的东西的地方。
- **洒水器**是任何农场的即插即用配件：挂起来、供上水，下方的耕地永远湿润。
- 温室玻璃配有同款玻璃门；在季节模组眼里，它就是一间合格的温室——季节性作物在室内照常生长。
